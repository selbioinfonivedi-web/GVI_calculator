GVI Colour System

STAIN · A COLOUR ARCHITECTURE FOR GENOMIC ANALYSIS · v1.0

Hues are derived from molecular-biology stains — methylene blue, ethidium, malachite green, crystal violet, eosin, safranin — rather than from a screen palette. Two rules govern every application: colour states what kind of claim a pixel is making, and finish states how far that claim can be trusted. Every value below was run through a six-check validator (lightness band, chroma floor, CVD separation, normal-vision floor, contrast) on its own ground; the results are quoted, not asserted.

03 · SIGNATURE — the platform's own colour

phosphorLive instrument state: cursor, active readout, selection edge#17A89C#00776B
Phosphor is reserved for the interface, never for data. It marks what is live: the cursor, the active readout, the selected edge. Testing showed it sits only ΔE 12.9 from series blue under all-pairs — so it was removed from the data palette entirely rather than allowed to impersonate a series.

06 · TRUST FINISH — the system's distinguishing move

Colour encodes biology; finish encodes whether the number can be trusted. This keeps the two orthogonal, so a gated measurement never has to give up its identity hue to say it was gated.

08 · SELECTION PRESSURE — diverging, grey midpoint

Purifying ↔ neutral ↔ positive. Two poles that read as opposite, with a neutral midpoint so “no selection” looks like nothing rather than like a third category.

10 · RULES

Cool is measured, warm is inferred. A warm hue on screen always means a model produced it. If a reader has to ask whether a number was observed or fitted, the colour has failed.
Finish carries trust, hue carries identity. Never recolour a gated value — hatch it. Its lineage identity is still true.
Status colours are reserved. They are never slot 8 of a series, and never appear without their glyph.
Phosphor is interface-only. If teal appears in a chart, something is wrong.
Three slots for all-pairs forms, seven for adjacent forms. Beyond the cap, fold to “Other” or facet — never generate an extra hue.
Dark and light are one product. Same hue family, same slot order, re-stepped for each ground. Light mode is not an inverted dark mode: greens and teals gain chroma on paper or they read grey, which is exactly what the validator caught twice here.
No gradient grounds, no decorative shadow. Depth comes from the six background values and hairlines. A shadow appears only on a floating console, where it signals detachment from the plane.
