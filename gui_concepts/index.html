Three full dashboards

All three are complete application screens driven by the same real corpus — 16 pathogens, 144 index slots, 40 gated exclusions. Same charts, same data; what differs is the organising principle.

1 · Analyst Workbench

Light, sidebar-driven, dense. Corpus overview and per-dataset detail on one screen, with KPI tiles, the scatter, the availability matrix, the gating panel and the full table.

familiar · scales to more views 2 · Control Room

Dark, twelve-column grid, no navigation at all. Every panel visible simultaneously; run inputs and the gating log sit permanently beside the numbers they explain.

densest · best for batch monitoring 3 · Evidence Brief

Editorial. Opens with a written lede stating the finding, then charts as evidence, then a “what to fix” section where each exclusion becomes a button. Tabs instead of a sidebar.

most explanatory · closest to a write-up
