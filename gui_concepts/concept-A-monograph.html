Why these were dropped

The alignment is 38% gap characters and the mean mutation burden is 38% of its length, so the sequences are not the closely-related, genuinely-aligned set these position-wise indices assume. This is usually partial coverage rather than misalignment — re-running MAFFT will not help, because the aligner already placed the sequences correctly. Trim to the shared coverage window instead.
