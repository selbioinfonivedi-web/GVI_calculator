What to fix

Grouped by cause — five indices failing on one bad alignment is one problem to fix, not five.

01

Alignment coverage — 5 datasets

Sequences cover different windows, so position-wise indices compare real bases against absent data. This is partial coverage, not misalignment: re-running MAFFT will not help, because the aligner already placed them correctly.

02

No temporal signal — 6 datasets

The date-randomization test failed: shuffled collection dates reproduce the fitted rate, so it reflects lineage structure rather than elapsed time.

03

Re from the weak fallback — 2 datasets

The lineages-through-time estimator returns ~1.0 for every pathogen and cannot carry the largest weight in the scheme.
